Add DTF Club to your home screen

It opens like an app, with no App Store. Do this once.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Open https://namkurd.github.io in Safari or Chrome. (If you tapped a link in Slack, Messages or another app, open it in Safari or Chrome first. The browser built into those apps can't add it.)
  2. Tap the Share button. In Safari it's the square with an arrow at the bottom; in Chrome it's in the address bar at the top right.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Chrome needs iOS 16.4 or later for this. If you don't see Add to Home Screen in Chrome, use Safari.

Android

  1. Open https://namkurd.github.io in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).

Tips

  • Use the bottom bar to move between tools. The ‹ button goes back and ⟳ refreshes.
  • If something looks out of date, tap ⟳.
